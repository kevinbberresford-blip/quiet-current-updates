These processed recordings are dedicated under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): [Deep Sea by Umplix](https://opengameart.org/content/deep-sea), [First Light Particles by Yoiyami](https://opengameart.org/content/first-light-particles-%E2%80%93-cc0-atmospheric-pianoambient-track), [rain 1.wav by FenrirFangs](https://freesound.org/people/FenrirFangs/sounds/231687/), [Singing Bowl singing by steffcaffrey](https://freesound.org/people/steffcaffrey/sounds/449950/), [Singingbowl 108062.mp3 by smilecat77](https://freesound.org/people/smilecat77/sounds/475307/), and [Relaxation Sound by Kevin Manickam](https://freesound.org/people/Kevin_Manickam/sounds/514386/). The OpenGameArt sources are WAV originals; the Freesound sources are public transcoded MP3 previews. Source hashes and transformations are in [equalizer-sources.json](sound-sources/equalizer-sources.json) and [relaxation-source.json](sound-sources/relaxation-source.json).
